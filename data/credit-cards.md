## Foreign currency transactions

A **3.0% foreign currency conversion fee** applies to any transaction processed in a currency other than AUD, including online purchases from overseas merchants.

## Disputes and chargebacks

If you don't recognise a transaction, or a merchant fails to deliver goods or services, you can raise a dispute:

- **Disputes must be raised within 60 days** of the statement date on which the transaction appears.
- Lodge the dispute in the Northwind app (Card → Recent transactions → Dispute) or by calling 1800 NORTHWIND.
- Provisional credit is typically applied within 5 business days while the dispute is investigated.
- Most disputes are resolved within 45 days.

## Lost or stolen cards

Lock the card immediately in the Northwind app. A replacement card is dispatched within 2 business days at no charge.
