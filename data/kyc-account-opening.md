# Northwind Bank — KYC & Account Opening

> ⚠️ Fictional content for demo purposes only. Not financial advice.

## Who can open an account

- Individuals aged 18+ who are Australian residents for tax purposes.
- Joint applicants (up to 4) — all must complete identity verification.
- Sole traders and registered companies via the **Northwind Business** onboarding flow (separate process).

## Identity verification (100-point check)

Northwind Bank is required to verify your identity under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006. You must provide documents totalling **100 points**:

| Document | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Australian passport (current) | 70 |
| Australian driver licence | 40 |
| Medicare card | 25 |
| Australian birth certificate | 70 |
| Citizenship certificate | 70 |
| Recent utility bill (≤ 3 months old, matching address) | 25 |

You can complete the check digitally in the Northwind app using your driver licence and a live selfie, which usually takes **under 5 minutes**.

## Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) and sanctions screening

All new applicants are screened against PEP and global sanctions lists. If a positive match is returned, your application will be referred for enhanced due diligence and may take an additional 3 – 5 business days.

## Tax residency

You will be asked for your Tax File Number (TFN) and to declare any foreign tax residency under FATCA / CRS. Providing your TFN is optional but withholding tax will be applied to interest earned if it is not supplied.

## Turnaround

For straightforward applications, accounts are opened and BSB / account numbers issued within **1 – 2 business days** of all documents being received.
