# Northwind Bank — Savings & Term Deposits

> ⚠️ Fictional content for demo purposes only. Not financial advice.

## High-Interest Saver

A flexible, at-call savings account.

- **Base rate:** 1.10% p.a.
- **Bonus rate:** an additional **3.75% p.a.** for any month in which you:
  - Deposit at least AUD 200, **and**
  - Make no withdrawals.
- **Maximum balance earning bonus rate:** AUD 250,000.
- No monthly account fees.

## Term Deposits

Lock in a fixed rate for a fixed term. Minimum opening deposit AUD 5,000.

| Term | Rate (p.a.) |
| --- | --- |
| 3 months | 4.25% |
| 6 months | 4.55% |
| **12 months** | **4.85%** |
| 24 months | 4.65% |
| 36 months | 4.50% |

Interest is paid at maturity by default. Monthly or quarterly interest payment options are available on terms of 12 months or longer.

## Breaking a term deposit

You can request to break a term deposit before maturity. A **31-day notice period** applies, and the interest rate paid will be reduced according to the following schedule:

- Less than 25% of term elapsed: 20% of contracted rate
- 25% – 50% of term elapsed: 40% of contracted rate
- 50% – 75% of term elapsed: 60% of contracted rate
- 75%+ of term elapsed: 80% of contracted rate

## Government guarantee

All Northwind deposits are covered by the Australian Financial Claims Scheme up to AUD 250,000 per account holder.
