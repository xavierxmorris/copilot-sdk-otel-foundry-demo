# Northwind Bank — Personal Loans

> ⚠️ Fictional content for demo purposes only. Not financial advice.

## Eligibility

To apply for a Northwind Bank personal loan, you must:

- Be aged 18 or older and an Australian resident.
- Have a **minimum gross annual income of AUD 40,000**.
- Hold a Northwind transaction or savings account that has been open for at least 30 days.
- Have no unresolved defaults on your credit file in the last 24 months.

## Loan products

| Product | Amount | Term | Indicative APR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unsecured personal loan | AUD 5,000 – AUD 50,000 | 1 – 7 years | from **8.99% p.a.** for a 3-year term |
| Secured car loan | AUD 10,000 – AUD 100,000 | 1 – 7 years | from **6.49% p.a.** |
| Debt consolidation loan | AUD 5,000 – AUD 75,000 | 2 – 7 years | from **9.49% p.a.** |

Comparison rates and the rate you are offered depend on your individual circumstances, including your credit score.

## Fees

- **Establishment fee:** AUD 195 (one-off, financed into the loan).
- **Monthly service fee:** AUD 10.
- **Early repayment:** no fee on variable-rate loans; a break cost may apply to fixed-rate loans repaid early.

## Application

Apply in the Northwind app or at any branch. Conditional approval typically takes **1 – 2 business days** after submitting payslips and ID.
