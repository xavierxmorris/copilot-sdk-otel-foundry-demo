# Northwind Bank — Online Security & Fraud

> ⚠️ Fictional content for demo purposes only. Not financial advice.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Northwind Bank requires MFA on every login to internet banking and the mobile app. Supported second factors:

- Push notification to a registered Northwind mobile app (recommended).
- One-time SMS code (fallback only — being phased out by December 2026).
- FIDO2 security key (Platinum customers and business accounts).

You can register up to **3 trusted devices** per profile.

## Recognising scams

Northwind staff will **never**:

- Ask you for your full PIN, password, or one-time code.
- Ask you to move money to a "safe account" — there is no such thing.
- Send you a link by SMS asking you to "verify" or "release" a transaction.

If you receive a message that looks like it is from Northwind but you are unsure, **hang up or do not click the link**, and call us back on the number printed on the back of your card.

## Reporting fraud

If you believe you have been scammed, or you spot an unauthorised transaction on your account:

1. **Lock your card immediately** in the Northwind app.
2. **Report the fraud within 24 hours** via the app (Help → Report fraud) or by calling 1800 NORTHWIND.
3. Provide as much detail as you can (date, amount, merchant, any messages received).

**Fraud reimbursement policy:** if you report unauthorised activity within **24 hours** of it occurring, and you have not shared your security details, Northwind will reimburse the affected amount in full, typically within **5 business days**. Reports made after 24 hours are still investigated but reimbursement may be partial.

## Account alerts

Customers can enable free real-time alerts for:

- Any transaction over a configurable threshold (default AUD 100).
- Logins from a new device or location.
- Changes to contact details or registered devices.

We strongly recommend enabling all three.
